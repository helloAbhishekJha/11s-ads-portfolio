## Executive takeaways (from PDF)

- Buyers pay for **documented workflows** (~2 h/week), **custom avatar/voice** (brand), and **high-RPM niches** — not generic clip spam.
- Valuation ballpark: **24×–48×** monthly net profit (Flippa / Empire Flippers style).
- Shorts RPM (2026 estimates in PDF): Finance **$0.15–0.30** · Business/Tech **$0.12–0.25** · Story/Horror **$0.05–0.12** · Entertainment **$0.02–0.08** per 1k views.
- **Niche down** (e.g. “AI productivity” or “AI horror narrator”) — not “general AI.”

### Production chain (class)

1. Script / reel template → **scene-split JSON prompts** (no narration; SFX only — for **Veo 3.1**).
2. **9:16** frames · e.g. “3D Pixar” horror short recreate-from-frame prompt (in PDF).
3. Channel identity: large **PROMPT 1** in PDF — competitor research → name, description, tags, banner, **30-video starter plan** (use when you pick a niche).
4. Example channel name in PDF: **“Andhera cinema”** · profile pic **1080×1080**.
